Guest access, PacketFence style. With the RADIUS policy's unknown-device action set to Registration portal, an unknown device lands on the registration VLAN. There Panopticon hands out addresses (DHCP), answers every DNS name with the portal, and serves the registration page, so phones and laptops pop up their sign-in sheet. Once a guest is approved (at once, or by staff), Panopticon re-authorizes the device (CoA) onto the guest VLAN until the approval expires. {% call macros::icon("role") %} RADIUS / 802.1X
