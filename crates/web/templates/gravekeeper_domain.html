Delegated service account

Enter a least-privilege, delegated account (its userPrincipalName). It is validated on the write DC: it must be able to sign in and must not be a member of Domain Admins, Enterprise or Schema Admins, Administrators, or the Operators groups. Only after it passes is the password sealed (AES-256-GCM, ENCRYPTION_KEY) and stored, and the domain becomes writable. The password is sent once over the authenticated channel, piped to PowerShell over stdin, and never logged.
